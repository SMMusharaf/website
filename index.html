The Estate

Nine rooms, one hundred acres, and a factory that once smelled only of tea.

Wild Fern was built in 1932 as the bungalow of a tea planter, set high enough on Hantana's slopes to catch the cloud line most afternoons. We restored it in 2019 — keeping the original teak floors, the long verandas, the outline of the old tea factory, which now holds our lounge and library.

Everything beyond the garden wall still works: pickers cross the front lawn at dawn, the factory's rollers still turn twice a week, and the smell of withering leaf drifts in through the windows most evenings.
